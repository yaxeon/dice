# Roll the dice

A simple dice rolling app for phones and desktops.

- Roll 1, 2, or 3 dice with 4, 6, 8, or 10 sides.
- Tap, click, or drag to roll, or use Enter or Space on the keyboard.
- Watch dice follow the drag direction and settle with a rolling sound.
- Get independent random results with equal probability for each side.
- Keep your selected dice count and number of sides between visits.
- Use screen reader support and reduced motion settings.
- Work offline after the first load and caching, and add the app to your home screen.
